# Privacy Policy
**Last Updated:** September 8, 2025

## 1. Data We Collect
When you use the Bot, we may collect and store:
- **Server (Guild) IDs, Names, and Channel IDs** – to remember your configuration settings.
- **User IDs** – only when submitting `/feedback` or `/bugreport` to identify the source of the report.
- **ESPN League Information** – league ID, season year, and ESPN cookies (`SWID` and `ESPN_S2`) needed to fetch your league’s data. These are stored securely and never shared publicly.

We do **not** collect message content, DMs, or other personal information beyond what is listed above.

## 2. How We Use the Data
We use this data to:
- Deliver weekly recaps, stats, and rankings.
- Handle feedback and bug reports.
- Maintain and troubleshoot Bot functionality.

## 3. Data Retention
- Server settings are stored until you remove the Bot or request deletion.
- Feedback and bug reports are stored for ongoing improvement unless deletion is requested.
- ESPN credentials are stored only as long as necessary for the Bot to function.

## 4. Data Sharing
We do **not** sell or share your data with third parties. Data may be disclosed only if required by law or to comply with Discord’s Terms of Service.

## 5. Your Rights
You may request deletion of your server’s data at any time by contacting us via GitHub or our support server. Removing the Bot from your server also deletes its configuration data.

## 6. Security
We use reasonable safeguards (such as environment variables and secure databases) to protect stored credentials and settings. However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be announced in the support server.

## 8. Contact
For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at:
